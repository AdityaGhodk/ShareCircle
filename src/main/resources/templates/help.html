Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ShareCircle.

1. What is ShareCircle?

ShareCircle is a platform where users can rent and share useful items with their community instead of purchasing everything.

2. How can I find an item?

Visit the Browse Items page, explore available items or use the search box to find a specific item.

3. How can I book an item?

Open the item, click Book Now, select your booking dates and complete the booking process.

4. How can I check my bookings?

Visit the My Bookings section to view your booking details and booking status.

5. What if an item is unavailable?

You cannot book an unavailable item. You can explore other items listed on ShareCircle.

6. How can I contact support?

Visit our Contact Us page and send your question or issue through the contact form.
