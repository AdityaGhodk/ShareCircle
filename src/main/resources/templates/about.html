About ShareCircle

Rent More. Share More. Waste Less. ShareCircle helps people access useful items without needing to buy everything themselves.

Who We Are

ShareCircle is a community-based item sharing and rental platform. Our goal is to make useful items accessible, affordable and easier to share. From books and electronics to sports equipment and furniture, users can explore items that suit their needs.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To help people save money by renting and sharing useful items instead of purchasing everything new.

Our Vision

To build a sharing community where resources are used responsibly and unnecessary waste is reduced.

How ShareCircle Works

01. Browse Items

Explore available books, electronics, sports items and more.

02. Choose an Item

Select the item you need and check its details and price.

03. Book Your Item

Choose your rental dates and submit your booking request.

04. Share & Save

Use items according to the agreed rental terms and save money.
